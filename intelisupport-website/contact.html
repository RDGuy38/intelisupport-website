Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our services and support

How quickly can you respond to emergencies?

We guarantee a response within 15 minutes for critical system failures and security incidents. Our 24/7 emergency hotline ensures immediate assistance when you need it most.

Do you provide on-site support?

Yes, we offer on-site support within the San Francisco Bay Area. For other locations, we provide comprehensive remote support that can resolve most issues without the need for physical presence.

What are your service level agreements?

Our SLAs vary by service package but typically include 99.9% uptime guarantee, defined response times, and resolution targets. We provide detailed SLA documentation with all service agreements.

Can you work with our existing IT infrastructure?

Absolutely. We work with all major technology platforms and can integrate with your existing systems. Our team will assess your current infrastructure and recommend the best approach.

Do you offer free consultations?

Yes, we provide free initial consultations to assess your IT needs and recommend appropriate solutions. This includes a basic security assessment and infrastructure review.

What industries do you serve?

We serve businesses across all industries including healthcare, finance, legal, manufacturing, retail, and professional services. Our solutions are customized to meet industry-specific requirements.
